About Anda Tayo

The problem

Students often struggle to find a hangout or study spot that fits what they need at that moment: a quiet place to focus, a budget-friendly spot to relax with friends, or a nice spot for taking photos. Generic map apps and word-of-mouth do not consider mood, weather, or how busy a place is, so students end up hopping from place to place before settling on one.

What Anda Tayo does

Anda Tayo helps students and locals in Zamboanga City quickly discover hangout and study spots that match their mood, needs, and situation. Spot details, mood-based filters, and real student reviews all live in one place.

StudentsFilter by mood, budget and distance, and read honest reviews.
Spot ownersShowcase a cafe or hangout and keep its details current.
Content creatorsScout spots by how photogenic they are, plus WiFi and sockets.

Verification rules

  • Anyone can submit a tambayan spot.
  • New submissions stay hidden until an admin checks the details and approves them.
  • Only admins can edit or delete spots.
