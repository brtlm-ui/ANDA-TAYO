# Demo photos

Taken from the Anda Tayo Figma mockup (AndaTayo — APP DEV) and resized to max 1200px JPEG.
`python manage.py seed_demo` attaches them to the matching demo spots as cover photos.

These are placeholder images for the class project. Several appear to be from the web, not taken
by the team — for example:

- `dennis-coffee-garden.jpg` — watermark "@thepoortraveler"
- `paseo-del-mar.jpg`, `coffee-spot.jpg` — watermark "Images of Zamboanga Peninsula"

Before putting the site online for the public, replace them with your own photos or ask the
owners for permission and credit them.
